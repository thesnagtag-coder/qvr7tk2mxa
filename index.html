    What is happening right now? Tap it for calm steps, words to say, and what you and the other person might be struggling with.

    Presence basics

    • Pause for 2 seconds before you answer.
    • Speak slower and lower than you feel like.
    • Use fewer words. Say it once, then stop.
    • Relax your jaw, face and shoulders.
    • Calm, comfortable eye contact. No rushing.
    • Ask questions and really listen.
    • Warm and firm together: kind words, clear limits.
    Hard to read does not mean hiding your feelings. Feel them fully later, in your notes or with someone you trust. In the moment, you choose what to show.
    If you ever feel unsafe, leave the situation and contact someone you trust or your local emergency number.

        How to use it

        • Each day has 4 tabs: Stoic, Manifest, Gratitude and Mindful. Do all 4, one at a time.
        • Morning: Stoic quote, Manifest intention and Mindful focus. Evening: Gratitude and your notes.
        • Do the small "Try today" task. Tick it when done.
        • In the evening, write a line or two in your notes.
        • Start the morning with the Morning mind practice (5 minutes).
        • Feeling the rush to react? Tap Pause first.

        Being unbothered is not being a doormat

        Stoic calm means you choose your response instead of reacting. It still includes boundaries, saying no, and stepping away from people who keep hurting you (see the "Boundaries" entry). If someone's behaviour affects your safety or health, please talk to someone you trust or a professional.

        About the Mindful tab

        The Mindful tab gives general habits for mindful eating and cravings. It is not medical or diet advice. If you have a health condition or take medication, check with your doctor before making big changes to how you eat.
